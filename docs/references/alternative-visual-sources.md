# Alternative visual sources: independent GitHub projects

Inspected 2026-11-01. This is a reference audit, not a game change. The two repositories were already downloaded to `.local/research/sources/`; their local JPEG files were opened directly for visual inspection. No Fandom page, proxy relay, or archive relay was used for this audit.

The strongest alternate cavern reference is **CentrinoPanda's `Caverns.jpg`**. It resembles a Slugterra production image and contains a partial star/“N” mark. **King-Koi's `Cueva.jpg`** also visibly contains Slugterra-related slug imagery, but looks like game artwork or a composite. Neither repository supplies a traceable production credit for those images. The other backgrounds are generic or unidentified fantasy illustrations and do not establish Slugterra canon.

## CentrinoPanda/SlugterraInfo

### `Digi Tech/Caverns.jpg` — useful, production provenance incomplete

[View the exact image file](https://github.com/CentrinoPanda/SlugterraInfo/blob/5c4c66b7aa35c6e05321dd04d7a5026fb5940f21/Digi%20Tech/Caverns.jpg) · 1400 × 734 pixels

Direct visual observations:

- An enclosed, high cavern with an overhead stalactite silhouette and distant pale cyan rock walls; no open sky is visible.
- Dense stands of giant mushrooms form the dominant scenery. Broad purple caps carry pink speckles, with orange-red cap colonies interspersed.
- The gills beneath the caps glow turquoise, lighting nearby stems and the ground. Foreground caps crop against the frame, making the mushrooms feel very large.
- Deep violet, blue, and near-black stems bend, flare, and fork; these are organic silhouettes rather than straight poles.
- A relatively open, gently winding teal ground corridor leads between the growth. Smaller plants and sparse warm orange objects provide scale and color accents.
- Blue-green haze and repeated mushroom layers create depth. The image is not predominantly a field of giant crystals.

**Confidence:** high that this is a coherent stylized CG mushroom-cavern image; medium that it is Slugterra production imagery. The fan site's surrounding Slugterra context and the partial star/“N” watermark at lower right support that interpretation. They do not identify an episode, artist, location name, or original publication. Do not label it as a verified named cavern or generalize its biome to all 99 caverns.

[Story.html](https://github.com/CentrinoPanda/SlugterraInfo/blob/5c4c66b7aa35c6e05321dd04d7a5026fb5940f21/Digi%20Tech/Story.html) embeds `Caverns.jpg`, but its story body is placeholder nonsense text. It adds no useful production attribution or lore verification.

### Attribution and lore limits

The repository's [Image Source & Attribution.txt](https://github.com/CentrinoPanda/SlugterraInfo/blob/5c4c66b7aa35c6e05321dd04d7a5026fb5940f21/Digi%20Tech/Image%20Source%20%26%20Attribution.txt) credits **upklyak / Freepik** and links a [moon-and-mountain-cleft night vector](https://www.freepik.com/free-vector/mountains-cleft-view-from-bottom-night-scenery-landscape-with-high-rocks-full-moon-with-stars-glowing-peaks_13194970.htm). That credit describes the site's generic star/mountain artwork. It does **not** specifically credit `Caverns.jpg` or establish that the same artist made it. No separate license or production credit for the cavern image was found.

[Origin.html](https://github.com/CentrinoPanda/SlugterraInfo/blob/5c4c66b7aa35c6e05321dd04d7a5026fb5940f21/Digi%20Tech/Origin.html) gives an informal fan summary mentioning Eli, the Shane Gang, Dr. Blakk/dark water, and 99 caverns. Its spelling, release-history assertions, and lack of supporting citations make it unsuitable as a sole source for detailed canon or dates. Treat it as a fan synopsis that needs corroboration.

### Region-name caution

The fan project uses **Alta Cueva Caverns, Civilized Eastern Caverns, Magma Caverns, Northern Caverns, The Outland Reach, The Shadow Mines, Deep Swamp Caverns, Tropical Caverns, and Wild Western Caverns** as table groupings. Some terms may overlap with Slugterra geography, but this repository does not substantiate that relationship. Treat them as the fan RPG's organizational labels unless an independent canonical source confirms each name and its geography. Do not infer that the attached generic artwork depicts that named place in the show.

## Useful conclusions for later visual planning

1. Use `Caverns.jpg` as the clearest **provisional visual reference** for an enclosed Slugterra-like mushroom forest: purple and red caps, turquoise gills, twisted trunks, layered ceiling silhouettes, and an open ground corridor.
2. Use `Cueva.jpg` only as supporting **Slugterra-related game-art context**, keeping its composite/game presentation separate from show imagery.
3. Keep the remaining files in a separate generic inspiration category. Their palette or composition can be described, but they cannot verify canon, region names, flora, or world history.
4. These files were inspected as research references only. No images were copied into game assets, no game code was changed, and the incomplete provenance does not establish asset reuse rights.
