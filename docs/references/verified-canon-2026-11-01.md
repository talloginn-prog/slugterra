# Verified Slugterra references — 1 November 2026

This continuation uses the existing game, its assets and recorded audio. Research distinguishes official source facts, secondary Wiki leads and original game design. [Reference asset provenance](reference-assets.json) records exact source-image URLs, relative repository paths, dimensions, hashes and visual observations; [species source data](canonical-source-catalog.json) keeps classification evidence separate from balancing.

## Primary anchors

The [official Slugterra site](https://www.slugterra.com/) describes a luminous, high-tech underground adventure with living slugs and links to the [official Slugterra–WildBrain channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Slugterra). [WildBrain’s Slugslinger Showdown announcement](https://www.wildbrain.com/trade-news/dhx-media-unleashes-slugslinger-showdown) directly establishes exploration, collecting/training slugs, duels, gear upgrades, transformation when fired at **100 MPH**, and Dr. Blakk’s ghoul-corruption threat. Numeric damage, return timing and RPG progression are not supplied by that synopsis.

[WildBrain’s season-four announcement](https://www.wildbrain.com/trade-news/family-chrgd-slugs-it-out-with-the-season-four-premiere-of-slugterra-october-4) confirms the **99 caverns**, the Shane Gang’s Eastern adventure, Gateway Cavern and a conflict involving the Shadow Clan’s priorities for slugs and people. It does not provide a complete geographic map, travel graph or region dimensions.

The current official site includes later shorts and licensed games. Newer species and game variants should not be silently treated as original-series canon. Named individual slugs and species are distinct concepts: Burpy is an Infurnus; Joules is a Tazerling; Beeker is a named AquaBeek.

## Slug Fu

The licensed [Apple TV film synopsis](https://tv.apple.com/us/movie/slugterra-slug-fu-showdown/umc.cmc.54sblq1fo7zjvegkialxf7yv1) and [Prime Video synopsis](https://www.primevideo.com/-/fil/detail/0GJSIJS4UO3J3C9XQFSS1SOJVI?tr=us) directly describe Slug Fu as guiding and controlling a slug in battle and identify Junjie teaching Eli. Training prerequisites, resource costs, turn rate, duration and cooldown remain game design choices. Generic dashes, healing buttons or damage multipliers are not established Slug Fu techniques by these sources.

## Characters, society and economy

The [ABC broadcaster still](https://iview.abc.net.au/show/slugterra/series/1/video/ZX9896A004S00) provides actual ensemble appearance and relative-scale reference for Eli, Trixie, Kord and Pronto. It supports stylized proportions, distinct silhouettes and recurring equipment motifs; it supplies no character statistics or dialogue.

Secondary indexed Wiki leads identify [Redhook](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Redhook) as a blastersmith, [Barton and Spinks](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Barton_and_Spinks) as a shop, and [gold coins](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Gold) as currency. Exact episodes and shop interactions still need direct footage verification. No authoritative in-world RPG price list was recovered. Prices, ownership rules, rewards, trainer costs and stock behavior are original balancing. Real USD toy prices and licensed mobile-game gem economies do not establish those prices.

## Original game content and preservation

**The Luminous Deep, Haven Outpost, Ranger Kael, Luminous Grove, Violet Falls, Ancient Roots, the Heartstone, heartshards and Hollow Warden** are the existing project’s original content. Retain their playable quest and saved progress; do not relabel them as canonical show locations or characters. Player levels, regenerating shield, companion cooldowns, 1.6× morph damage and numeric rewards are also project mechanics. New connected-cavern geography and dialogue are adaptations even when a location name is sourced.

Keep the original three equipped-slot controls and companion IDs. A larger owned collection is separate from those slots. Keep the supplied transformation MP3, its audio-slot IDs, 85ms playback offset and browser-local audio override storage independent of campaign saves and content catalogs.
